1) Herd Overview (at-a-glance)
- Total Animals (current): count by species.
- Archived animals summary. (from ArchivedAnimal model)
- Youngstock: < 1 year (age from DoB), (calves). only cattle
- Herd Growth This Month: births − deaths. (cattle only)
- Data Quality Score: % animals with photo + valid IDs (E-ID, V-ID) + species + sex + DoB present.
- Visuals: KPI tiles + donut by species + small trend sparkline (30/90 days).

2) Animal Structure & Demographics

- Counts by species, breed, sex, age bands (e.g., 0–6m, 6–12m, 12–24m, >24m). (for cattle only)
- Parity Distribution (if tracked via calving history): heifers vs. parity 1/2/3+. (for cattle only)
- Heifers Near First Service: females of breeding age (e.g., >14m cattle) without service record. (for cattle only)
Visuals: stacked bar (breed within species), age pyramid, parity bars. (for cattle only)

3) Registration & Data Completeness

- Animals Missing Photo / Tag.

- Duplicate IDs Detected (same E-ID/V-ID).

- Offline Backlog: animals not submitted, photos not uploaded, sessions not submitted (you already track these).

- Average Time to Upload: from local save to successful upload.

- Visuals: bars + warning chips with “Fix Now”.

4) Reproduction & Fertility (per species; especially cattle/goats/sheep)

- Services in Period (AI vs Natural).

- Conception Rate = # Pregnant / # Services.

- Services per Conception (S/C) = # Services / # Pregnancies.

- Return-to-Estrus Rate = % serviced again within 21–24 days after service.

- Pregnancy Check Coverage = # animals checked / # eligible.

- PD Results Breakdown: Pregnant vs Not Pregnant vs Retest.

- Expected Calvings (calendar): from service_date + gestation or pregnancy_delivery_expected_date.

- Defaults: cattle ~283d, goats ~150d, sheep ~152d, pigs ~115d (adjustable species table).

- Calving Interval (CI) per cow = date_of_current_calving − date_of_previous_calving.

- Days Open (for lactating cows) = service_conception_date − calving_date.

- Visuals: funnels (service→PD→pregnant), calendar of due calvings, histogram of CI, S/C by month.

5) Calving & Youngstock

- Calvings in Period: total and by sex of calf.

- Abortion Rate = # abortions / # pregnant animals.

- Average Calf Birth Weight.

- Weaning Rate = # weaned / # born for cohort.

- Average Weaning Age (days).

- Average Weaning Weight; Pre-weaning Mortality.

- Visuals: cohort funnel (born→weaned), box plots of birth & weaning weights.

6) Milk Production (dairy)

- Total Milk Produced (day/week/month).

- Average per Lactating Cow per Day = total milk / # lactating cows.

- 7-Day & 30-Day Moving Averages.

- Peak Yield this lactation and date.

- Days in Milk (DIM) Distribution.

- Milking Records Coverage: milking events per lactating animal per day (completeness).

- Visuals: daily line with MAs, bars by group/farm, violin for DIM vs production.

7) Growth & Weights

Recent Weighings: coverage = # animals weighed / # animals targeted.

Average Daily Gain (ADG) by cohort/species/breed =
(weight₂ − weight₁) / (days_between_weighings).

% On-Target Weight: animals within target band for age/breed.

Growth Outliers: animals with negative or very low ADG.

Visuals: scatter (age vs weight), ADG distribution histograms.

8) Health & Disease (Sanitary Events)

Disease Incidence Rate = new cases / population at risk (per 100 head-month).

Top 5 Diseases (cases, incidence).

Case Fatality Rate per disease = deaths among cases / cases.

Time to Recovery = recovery_date − onset_date (if available).

Recurrence Rate (repeat cases on same animal within 30/60/90 days).

Visuals: heatmap by disease x month, trendlines for key diseases.

9) Vaccination & Biosecurity

Coverage by Disease (e.g., FMD, PPR, LSD, etc.) =
# vaccinated (within valid window) / # eligible
(eligibility by species, age, and dose schedule).

Overdue Vaccinations count and list.

Booster Due in Next 30/60 Days.

Last FMD Date by animal; % with current FMD status.

Visuals: coverage bars per disease + calendar of due boosters.

10) Treatments & Drug Inventory

From Treatment events & DrugStockBatch/VetDrug models

Treatments by Disease & Outcome (if outcome captured).

Average Dose per Animal vs recommended (if dose rules exist).

Drug Stock On Hand by item.

Days of Stock Remaining = current_quantity / avg_daily_usage.

Soon-to-Expire (within 30/60 days).

Stockouts & Near-Stockouts thresholds.

Consumption by Disease/Program (which diseases consume most).

Visuals: stock cards with progress bars; expiry warnings; Pareto (80/20) of consumption.

11) Mortality & Culling

Mortality Rate = deaths / average headcount (period).

By Cause (from Mortality event notes/categories).

Age at Death distribution; Early-Life Mortality (e.g., <30d calves).

Culling Count & Reasons (if recorded).

Visuals: cause breakdown pie; age-at-death histogram.

12) Productivity & Simple Economics (optional but useful)

(You can compute with assumed prices or from orders/sales if you have them)

Estimated Milk Revenue = milk_qty × price (configurable milk price).

Cost of Treatments = sum(drug unit cost × qty) if costs stored.

Cost per Liter (if feed/costs exist; else show treatment cost/liter).

Calf Value Added: count of calves × assumed value per species/breed.

Visuals: KPI tiles; trendlines of revenue proxies.

13) Workflows & Backlog (Ops Health)

Unsubmitted Sessions, Local Animals Not Submitted, Photos Not Uploaded (already shown).

Average Sync Latency.

Data Entry Completeness (% events with required fields filled).

Visuals: chips + SLA gauges.

14) Schedules, Forecasts & Task List

- Due Today/This Week:

- Vaccinations due (by disease).

- Pregnancy Checks due (e.g., 30–45 days post-service).

- Expected Calvings (calendar).

- Weighing due (e.g., monthly/quarterly).

- Weaning due (based on wean rules or entered wean dates).

- Dry-off due (DIM-based rule).

- Overdue Items with days overdue.

- Visuals: agenda list + calendar heatmap.

 
17) Alerts & Anomalies (actionable)

- Milk Drop Alert: ≥15% drop vs 7-day average for any cow/herd.

- Spike in Abortions: above baseline/threshold.

- High Disease Incidence: exceed control threshold (configurable).

- Drug Near Expiry / Low Stock.

- Data Quality Alerts: duplicate ID, missing fields on active animals.

- Overdue Vaccinations / PD / Weighing / Weaning.

- Visuals: colored alert list with severity and “Go to list” links.

18) Benchmarks & Comparisons (optional but motivating)

Farm vs Last Month / Last Year (milk, fertility, mortality).

Farm vs District Average (if you compute peer aggregates).

Top/Bottom 10% animals by milk, ADG, health events.

Visuals: variance bars, leaderboards.

Key Backend Computations (mapping to your models)

Population & cohorts

Headcount on date = animals with no mortality/slaughter event before date.

Age = today − dob.

Lactating = calving_date set AND (no dry-off event OR DIM below threshold).

Reproduction

Conception: PD result = Pregnant.

CI = difference between consecutive calving dates per animal.

Expected calving = service_date + gestation_days (species map).

Days Open = from calving to conception date (first positive PD).

Milk

Sum item.milk from Milking events; per-animal & herd aggregations.

DIM = today − last calving_date.

Weights

Pairwise last two weights per animal to compute ADG; keep window (last 60–120 days).

Target bands via species/breed growth tables (configurable).

Health

Disease incidence per month = new cases / avg headcount * 100.

Case fatality = deaths among cases / total cases.

Vaccination

Coverage = % with valid vaccination within schedule window.

Due/overdue via next due date per protocol.

Drugs/Inventory

Days of stock remaining = current_qty / avg_daily_usage (rolling 30 days).

Near-expiry if expiry_date − today <= threshold.

Backlog

Counts from local tables: ImageModelLocal, AnimalModelLocal, SessionModel where status != uploaded.
